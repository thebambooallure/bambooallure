WHO WE ARE

We’re not a big company — and that’s the way we are.

We stay personal, work closely with you and treat your home like our own.

“Different Root, One Team, One Promise”

We are a small group of people from North East India, Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan, brought together in Namma Bengaluru with a shared passion for delivering quality craftsmanship.

From woodwork to electrical, false ceiling to painting, we take care of your interior work end to end—with a personal touch.

WHY US

We don't believe in simply taking on your project.

Bengaluru has no shortage of interior & modular factory companies, but as the industry has grown, the focus on quality and personal involvement can sometimes get diluted.

Many businesses in Bengaluru focus on taking up more projects and then rely heavily on external vendors for execution and this creates multiple layers between the customer and the people actually doing the work.

At Bamboo Allure, we take a more accountable approach.

We believe that when we take up a project, we should be responsible for delivering it—not simply passing it on.

That's why we work with our own experienced and trusted team to execute the work we commit to. Whether it is woodwork, electrical, false ceiling, painting or other interior work, our focus remains on completing the job properly and keeping the customer satisfied.

FAQ

Questions you may have

What interior work does Bamboo Allure handle?

We take care of interior work end to end, including woodwork, modular kitchens, wardrobes, storage units, TV units, false ceilings, electrical work, painting, pooja units, quartz, granite, tiles work and more.

Do I have one point of contact throughout my project?

Yes. The person who discusses your requirements and helps you plan the work stays connected throughout the execution.

Do you use your own team for execution?

We work with our own experienced and trusted team to execute the work we commit to.

How do I start a project with Bamboo Allure?

Start with a conversation through WhatsApp, phone, email or the enquiry form on this website.

Where is Bamboo Allure based?

Timelines depend on scope, materials and site requirements. We discuss the expected schedule after understanding your project.
